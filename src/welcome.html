Before you start

  • Ruby reads the text of the web pages you visit so it can find Japanese and show English or furigana above it.
  • This happens entirely on your device. Page text is never saved, logged or sent anywhere, and Ruby makes no network requests.
  • Ruby saves only your display settings, in your browser.

Until you agree, Ruby stays off. You can remove it any time at chrome://extensions.
